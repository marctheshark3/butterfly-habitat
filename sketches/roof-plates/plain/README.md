## Variant: plain roof

### Design stance
The photo corner is a bug, so the plate is a full mesh frame and nothing else.

### Key choices
- Layout: 200 mm square, 12 mm border, 176 mm window, clamp is a closed ring.
- The 48 by 40 mm shelf and the 8 mm hole are gone.
- Roof still lifts off. That is the hatch.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: closing the corner, giving the caterpillars mesh there, matching a no-camera build.
- Weak at: no place to put the XIAO without a new plate.

### Best for
The build in hand if the camera stays off.
